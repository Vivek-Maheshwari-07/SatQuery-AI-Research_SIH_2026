# SIH Judging Criteria & Evaluation Rubric

This document outlines the standard judging rubric and scoring dimensions used in Smart India Hackathon evaluations.

---

## ⚖️ Evaluation Dimensions

| Dimension | Typical Weightage | Evaluation Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Novelty & Innovation** | High | Agentic orchestration, evidence-grounded VQA, multimodal Optical+SAR fusion. |
| **Technical Feasibility & Architecture** | High | Sound system architecture, geospatial processing pipelines, model selection rationale. |
| **Clarity & Completeness of Solution** | High | Fulfilling all PS requirements: Single VQA, Change Detection, Grounding, Traceability. |
| **User Experience & Explainability** | Medium-High | Visual evidence overlays, confidence indicators, transparent step-by-step reasoning trace. |
| **Scalability & Domain Impact** | Medium | Handling multi-resolution GeoTIFFs, real-world utility for ISRO, disaster response, and urban planning. |
| **Presentation & Live Q&A** | Medium | Clear demonstration, solid defense of technical choices, team coordination. |

---

## 🏆 Key Differentiators for SatQuery AI

1. **True Multimodal Support**: Handling both multispectral optical bands and SAR data rather than generic 3-channel RGB.
2. **Transparent Agentic Pipeline**: Dynamic tool selection with verifiable execution trace rather than an ungrounded black-box LLM.
3. **Evidence-Grounded Answers**: Every natural language claim is cross-referenced with exact coordinates/bounding boxes or segmentation masks.
