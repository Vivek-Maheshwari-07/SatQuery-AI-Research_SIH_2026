# Problem Statement: PS-26167

## 🏛️ Problem Information

- **Problem Statement ID**: `26167`
- **Title**: SatQuery AI – An Interactive Vision-Language Assistant for Multimodal Remote Sensing Image Analysis through Text Queries
- **Organization**: Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)
- **Category**: Software
- **Theme**: Space Technology
- **Hackathon**: Smart India Hackathon 2026

---

## 🎯 Official Problem Summary

Modern Earth Observation (EO) satellites generate vast streams of high-resolution multimodal data, including optical/multispectral imagery, hyperspectral data, and Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) acquisitions. Extracting actionable insights from these datasets typically requires specialized geospatial knowledge, complex GIS software, and manual interpretation.

ISRO seeks an **Interactive Vision-Language Assistant** (SatQuery AI) that allows analysts, disaster management teams, urban planners, and defense personnel to query and analyze remote sensing imagery through natural language prompts. The system must orchestrate specialized deep learning models, fuse multimodal sensor data, perform temporal change detection, provide visual evidence (masks/bounding boxes), state confidence levels, and provide a clear step-by-step reasoning trace.

---

## 🛰️ Key Focus Areas

1. **Multimodal Remote Sensing Perception**:
   - Seamless interpretation of electro-optical (RGB, multispectral bands) and Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery.
   - Robustness to atmospheric conditions, cloud cover, sensor noise, and varying ground sampling distances (GSD).

2. **Natural Language Understanding & Agentic Execution**:
   - Parsing complex, compositional, and conversational geospatial queries.
   - Autonomous routing to domain-specific specialist vision models and geospatial analysis tools.

3. **Multi-Temporal & Change Reasoning**:
   - Bi-temporal image comparison to detect, localize, and semantically describe environmental and man-made changes.

4. **Explainability, Evidence & Confidence**:
   - Generating visual artifacts (heatmaps, bounding boxes, segmentation masks) corresponding to textual assertions.
   - Calibrated confidence estimation and verifiable step-by-step reasoning traces.
