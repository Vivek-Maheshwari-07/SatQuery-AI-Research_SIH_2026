# Remote Sensing Vision-Language Models (RS-VLMs)

## 📌 Purpose
This directory tracks literature on Vision-Language Models specifically pre-trained, fine-tuned, or adapted for remote sensing and Earth observation domains.

---

## 🎯 What Belongs Here
- Research papers on domain-specific RS-VLMs (e.g., GeoChat, EarthGPT, RSGPT, SkyEyeGPT, RemoteCLIP, LHRS-Bot).
- Comparative studies between general-domain VLMs and remote sensing fine-tuned foundation models.

## 🚫 What Does NOT Belong Here
- Generic computer vision papers without remote sensing relevance.
