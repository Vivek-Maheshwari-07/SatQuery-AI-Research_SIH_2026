# Optical & SAR Fusion Research

## 📌 Purpose
This directory tracks academic research on cross-modal fusion between electro-optical (multispectral/RGB) and Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery.

---

## 🎯 What Belongs Here
- Papers on pixel-level, feature-level, and decision-level optical-SAR fusion.
- Deep cross-attention and contrastive learning between SAR backscatter and optical spectral bands.
- Cloud removal and all-weather Earth observation frameworks using SAR guidance.
