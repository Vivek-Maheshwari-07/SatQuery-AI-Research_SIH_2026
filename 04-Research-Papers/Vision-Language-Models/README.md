# Vision-Language Models (General Domain)

## 📌 Purpose
This directory tracks literature on general-purpose vision-language foundation architectures, multimodal alignment mechanisms, and instruction-tuning paradigms.

---

## 🎯 What Belongs Here
- Foundational papers on architectures like CLIP, BLIP-2, InstructBLIP, LLaVA, Florence-2, Qwen-VL, and PaliGemma.
- Studies on visual tokenization, cross-attention projection, and prompt grounding.

## 🚫 What Does NOT Belong Here
- Pure NLP papers without vision components.
