# 10 - Team Research & Collaboration

## 📌 Purpose
This directory tracks internal team collaboration, meeting minutes, exploratory research notes, architectural decision records (ADRs), brainstorming logs, and active task backlogs for the SatQuery AI project.

---

## 📂 Contents

| File / Folder | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| [`Research-Notes/`](Research-Notes/README.md) | Individual team member research notes, experiment logs, and technical exploratory summaries. |
| [`Meeting-Notes/`](Meeting-Notes/README.md) | Agendas, discussion records, action items, and minutes from team syncs and mentor meetings. |
| [`Decisions.md`](Decisions.md) | Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) tracking major technical choices, trade-offs, and rationale. |
| [`Ideas.md`](Ideas.md) | Brainstorming repository for innovative features, UI concepts, and future extensions. |
| [`TODO.md`](TODO.md) | Prioritized checklist of active tasks, milestone deliverables, and research assignments. |

---

## 👥 How Team Members Should Use This
1. Log all team meeting decisions in `Meeting-Notes/`.
2. Document significant architectural choices in `Decisions.md`.
3. Update `TODO.md` as tasks are completed or new research needs are identified.
