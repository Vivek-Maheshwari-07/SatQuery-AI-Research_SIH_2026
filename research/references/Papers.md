# Research Papers Master Bibliography

This document provides a master bibliography of foundational papers relevant to SatQuery AI.

---

## 📚 Remote Sensing VLMs & Multimodal Assistants

1. **GeoChat**:
   - *Citation*: Khattak, M. U., et al. (2024). *"GeoChat: Grounded Large Vision-Language Model for Remote Sensing"*. CVPR 2024.
   - *Link*: https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.15826
   - *Code*: https://github.com/mbzuai-oryx/GeoChat

2. **EarthGPT**:
   - *Citation*: Zhang, X., et al. (2024). *"EarthGPT: A Universal Multi-modal Large Language Model for Multi-sensor Remote Sensing Land Comprehension"*. arXiv:2401.16822.
   - *Link*: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.16822

3. **RemoteCLIP**:
   - *Citation*: Liu, C., et al. (2024). *"RemoteCLIP: A Vision-Language Foundation Model for Remote Sensing"*. IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing (TGRS).
   - *Link*: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.11029

---

## 📚 Visual Question Answering (RS-VQA)

4. **RSVQA**:
   - *Citation*: Lobry, S., Marcos, D., Murray, J., & Tuia, D. (2020). *"RSVQA: Visual Question Answering for Remote Sensing Data"*. IEEE TGRS, 58(12), 8555-8566.
   - *Link*: https://doi.org/10.1109/TGRS.2020.2988868
   - *Portal*: https://rsvqa.sylvainlobry.com/

---

## 📚 Change Detection & Change Captioning

5. **ChangeFormer**:
   - *Citation*: Bandara, W. G. C., & Patel, V. M. (2022). *"A Transformer-Based Siamese Network for Change Detection"*. IEEE IGARSS 2022.
   - *Link*: https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.01293

6. **Bitemporal Image Transformer (BIT)**:
   - *Citation*: Chen, H., Qi, Z., & Shi, Z. (2022). *"Remote Sensing Image Change Detection With Transformers"*. IEEE TGRS.
   - *Link*: https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00208

---

## 📚 Multimodal Optical-SAR Fusion

7. **CROMA**:
   - *Citation*: Anthony, A., et al. (2023). *"CROMA: Cross-Modal Pre-Training for Remote Sensing"*. NeurIPS 2023.
   - *Link*: https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00566

8. **BigEarthNet-MM**:
   - *Citation*: Sumbul, G., et al. (2021). *"BigEarthNet-MM: A Large-Scale, Multimodal, Multilabel Benchmark Dataset for Remote Sensing Image Analysis"*. IEEE TGRS.
   - *Link*: https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.07921
