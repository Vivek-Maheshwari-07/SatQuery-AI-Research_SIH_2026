# Geospatial AI (GeoAI)

## 📖 Definition
Geospatial Artificial Intelligence (GeoAI) is an interdisciplinary field integrating artificial intelligence, spatial data science, geographic information systems (GIS), and remote sensing to extract knowledge, detect spatial patterns, and perform predictive modeling on spatial and spatiotemporal data.

---

## 🌟 Why It Matters
Spatial data exhibits distinct statistical properties governed by Tobler's First Law of Geography (*"everything is related to everything else, but near things are more related than distant things"*), spatial autocorrelation, spatial heterogeneity, and geographic coordinate projections. GeoAI models are engineered to respect these spatial geometries and topological relationships.

---

## 🔑 Important Concepts

1. **Spatial Representation**:
   - **Raster Data**: Continuous gridded field measurements (satellite pixels, elevation DEMs, temperature grids).
   - **Vector Data**: Discrete geometric entities (Points, MultiPoints, LineStrings, Polygons, MultiPolygons).

2. **Spatial Topology & Spatial Joins**:
   - Intersections, containment, buffering, convex hulls, and distance matrices between detected visual entities and reference GIS layers (e.g. administrative boundaries, road networks).

3. **Multi-Scale Spatial Tiling**:
   - Slippy map tile grids (XYZ / TMS), Web Map Services (WMS/WMTS), spatial indexing via R-Trees, H3 hexagonal hierarchical spatial indices, and S2 geometry.

---

## 🛠️ Common Techniques
- Zonal statistics (calculating mean/max spectral values within polygon zones).
- Vectorization of deep learning raster segmentation masks into GeoJSON / Shapefiles.
- Polygon area calculation over projected Coordinate Reference Systems (e.g., metric UTM).

---

## 🤖 Relevant Models & Tools
- **Libraries**: `shapely`, `geopandas`, `fiona`, `rasterio`, `pyproj`, `H3-py`, `PyDeck`, `Leaflet`, `MapLibre`.
- **Geospatial Foundations**: `TorchGeo`, `Clay Foundation Model`, `NASA-IBM Prithvi`.

---

## 🎯 Relevance to SatQuery AI
Ensures that visual evidence generated by SatQuery AI (e.g., change masks, grounding boxes) can be converted into georeferenced GIS vector layers, measure exact physical metric areas, and be seamlessly exported into standard GIS environments (QGIS, Bhuvan).
